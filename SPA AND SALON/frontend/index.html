WHY WE BUILT THIS

The front desk was the bottleneck.

01

No more hold music

Clients booked over the phone during business hours only — Verdant opens the calendar 24/7 from Firebase-backed availability.

02

Fewer no-shows

Firebase Cloud Messaging sends automatic reminders and lets clients reschedule themselves before a slot goes to waste.

03

Pay without the counter

Razorpay/Stripe checkout inside the app closes the loop — book, pay, and get a receipt in one flow.

ABOUT VERDANT

Started as a front-desk problem, not an app idea.

Our founding salon was turning away calls during peak hours because there was no one free to pick up the phone. Verdant began as a way to let clients see the real calendar and book straight into it — no waiting, no back-and-forth.

Today the same system runs booking, stylist schedules, and payments for the whole salon and spa floor.
